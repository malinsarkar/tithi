Privacy Policy

তিথি – বাংলা পঞ্জিকা

Effective date: August 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how তিথি – বাংলা পঞ্জিকা (referred to as "the App", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use our application. We built the App to bring the Bengali calendar, auspicious days (shubho din), government holiday lists, and festival dates to your device in a simple and reliable way. Protecting your privacy is important to us, and this policy describes our practices in plain terms.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

The App does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, or location in order to use it.

All calendar data, holiday information, and festival dates displayed within the App are either bundled with the App itself or calculated locally on your device. We do not have access to how you use the App, and we do not track your activity.

2. Permissions

The App may request only the minimum device permissions necessary for its core features to function correctly (for example, displaying notifications for important dates, if this feature is enabled). We do not use any permission to collect or share data with third parties.

3. Advertising

The App does not currently display any advertisements. The App does not integrate any advertising networks, and no ad-related data collection takes place at this time. If this changes in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly, and the update will be reflected here with a new effective date.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs. No data from your use of the App is shared with, or sold to, any third party.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since the App collects no personal data at all, it may be used safely by users of any age.

6. Data Security

Because the App does not collect or transmit personal data, there is no user data stored on any external server that could be exposed. All calendar and date calculations happen locally within the App on your device.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if new features (such as advertisements or optional account features) are introduced in the future. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
