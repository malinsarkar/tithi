Effective Date: August 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how তিথি – বাংলা পঞ্জিকা ("the App", "we", "our") handles information. Protecting your privacy is our top priority, and this policy describes our practices in plain, transparent terms.

1. Zero Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

The App does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personal details (like name, email, or location). All calendar data is calculated locally on your device.

2. Government Information & Disclaimer

The App provides general information regarding government holidays and festivals. These lists are sourced directly from official public portals, including:

  • Bangladesh National Portal: bangladesh.gov.bd
  • West Bengal Official Portal: wb.gov.in

Disclaimer: This App is strictly an independent platform. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any government entity. We do not represent any government agency.

3. App Permissions

The App only requests standard, minimal device permissions necessary for core features to function (such as triggering local reminders/notifications for festivals). No permissions are used to track your activity or share data.

4. Advertising & Third-Party Services

The App is completely ad-free and does not integrate any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs. Your app usage remains entirely private and is never shared with external servers.

5. Children's Privacy

Because the App collects absolutely no personal data, it is 100% safe for users of all ages, including children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly solicit data from or market to children.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, we would love to hear from you.
